Energy from capacity: An ideal cell is rated 2.35 Ah at a constant 3.7 V. Find its energy in Wh. Round to four significant figures.

Enter an answer, then check it.
Show worked solution

2.35 Ah × 3.7 V = 8.695 Wh. Ah alone is charge, not energy.

Watt-hours to joules: Convert 8.695 Wh to joules. Round to four significant figures.

Enter an answer, then check it.
Show worked solution

8.695 × 3600 = 31,302 J, or 31,300 J to four significant figures. A factor of 1000 converts kWh to Wh; it does not convert Wh to J.

Current from a discharge rating: A 2.35 Ah cell has a maximum continuous discharge rating of 4C. What is its rated current estimate?

Enter an answer, then check it.
Show worked solution

4 h⁻¹ × 2.35 Ah = 9.4 A. This is a rated limit, not a claim that the cell always supplies that current.

Ideal constant-current runtime: An ideal 4.7 Ah pack supplies a constant 1.8 A. Find runtime in hours, to four significant figures. Assume all rated charge is usable.

Enter an answer, then check it.
Show worked solution

4.7 Ah ÷ 1.8 A = 2.61111… h, or 2.611 h. That is about 156.7 minutes; enter hours.

Voltage of a 4s3p pack: Matched 3.6 V, 2.5 Ah cells form a 4s3p ideal pack: four cells per series string and three strings in parallel. Find pack voltage.

Enter an answer, then check it.
Show worked solution

4 × 3.6 V = 14.4 V. The three parallel strings do not multiply the voltage.

Capacity of a 4s3p pack: Matched 3.6 V, 2.5 Ah cells form a 4s3p ideal pack. Find pack capacity in Ah.

Enter an answer, then check it.
Show worked solution

3 × 2.5 Ah = 7.5 Ah. Series adds voltage; parallel adds charge capacity.

Energy of a 4s3p pack: Matched 3.6 V, 2.5 Ah cells form a 4s3p ideal pack. Find nominal energy in Wh.

Enter an answer, then check it.
Show worked solution

Pack voltage is 14.4 V and capacity is 7.5 Ah. Their product is 108 Wh. Check another way: 12 cells × 3.6 V × 2.5 Ah = 108 Wh.

Choose a primary cell: A clock is designed for a replaceable, non-rechargeable cell. Which category matches that requirement?

Enter an answer, then check it.
Show worked solution

Primary cells are intended for replacement after discharge, not recharging.

Choose a rechargeable battery: A portable instrument must be recharged repeatedly with a compatible charger. Which category is required?

Enter an answer, then check it.
Show worked solution

Secondary batteries are designed for repeated charging and discharging.

Storage using two reservoirs: A stationary storage project already has suitable upper and lower reservoirs. It proposes pumping water uphill and recovering energy downhill. Which method is described?

Enter an answer, then check it.
Show worked solution

Pumped hydro stores gravitational potential energy. This identifies the process; it does not establish a cost advantage at every site.

Storage for a portable instrument: A handheld instrument needs reusable electrical energy storage that travels with it. Which option fits?

Enter an answer, then check it.
Show worked solution

A rechargeable battery fits the portable load. Pumped hydro needs reservoirs and elevation difference.
