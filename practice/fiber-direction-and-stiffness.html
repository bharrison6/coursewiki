2. The axial compliance contains even powers of sine and cosine, so opposite angles have the same axial stiffness. Their extension–shear coupling is mirrored, so engineering shear strain changes sign.

Convert stress back to force

Convert stress to force: A patch has loaded cross-sectional area 200 mm² and uniform axial stress 10 MPa. Since 1 MPa = 1 N/mm², what total axial force corresponds to that stress?

Enter a number, then check it.
Show worked solution

F = σA = 10 N/mm² × 200 mm² = 2,000 N. Equal stress means equal force per area; total force changes if loaded area changes.
