Explain a no-HAZ choice

A conductive plate must retain an edge without thermal alteration. Explain why abrasive waterjet is a candidate and why EDM is not automatically a substitute.


Self-check: write first, then compare. Your response is not automatically graded or saved.

Show a model answer

Abrasive waterjet removes by mechanical erosion and normally produces no thermal HAZ. EDM removes by local melting and vaporization, so recast and thermal surface effects remain possible despite low cutting force. Waterjet edge quality and mechanical damage still need evaluation.

Distinguish the two liquids

Explain the difference between ECM electrolyte and EDM dielectric, including why “ECM makes no heat” is misleading.


Self-check: write first, then compare. Your response is not automatically graded or saved.

Show a model answer

ECM electrolyte conducts machining current and carries reaction products. EDM dielectric normally insulates until local breakdown permits a discharge, then helps cool and flush the gap. ECM does not melt the work, but current heats the electrolyte.

Thickness and finish

A waterjet profile has acceptable dimensions at the top but an unacceptable lower edge. Name two process effects to investigate.


Self-check: write first, then compare. Your response is not automatically graded or saved.

Show a model answer

Check taper (different cut widths through the thickness) and jet lag or striations. Thickness, travel speed and the cutting setup affect both. A no-HAZ process does not guarantee straight walls or the required surface finish.

Etching a narrow land

Why might a narrow strip of protected metal end up narrower than its mask pattern?


Self-check: write first, then compare. Your response is not automatically graded or saved.

Show a model answer

The etchant can attack sideways beneath the mask edge as well as downward. This undercut must be allowed for in mask design and limits small features, especially for deeper etches.

Wire center-path offset

Offset = radius + gap = 0.125 + 0.025 = 0.150 mm. The center path is on the removed-material side of the wall. 0.125 mm ignores the gap; 0.175 mm adds two gaps; 0.275 mm uses the wire diameter. The full kerf is 0.300 mm, not the one-sided offset.
