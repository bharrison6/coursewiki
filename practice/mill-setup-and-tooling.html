Stud near the part. A strap receives P = 7,200 N. The part-contact-to-stud distance a is 18 mm; the stud-to-heel distance b is 54 mm. What downward force does the strap apply to the part? Assume an ideal weightless strap with only part and heel contacts and a vertical stud load. Round to the nearest newton.

Enter a number, then check it.
Show reasoning

Fpart = P × b / (a + b) = 7,200 × 54 / (18 + 54) = 5,400 N. The heel receives 1,800 N. The two shares sum to 7,200 N. Using a in the numerator gives the heel share, not the requested part share.

Option 3. More of the circumference can become engaged in a corner. Smooth or reduce engagement using a suitable path and cutting data, then test the change.

Option 2. Speed changes the timing of tooth engagement. A different permitted speed may help, but the accompanying feed must still produce a suitable chip load. Slowing feed alone can cause rubbing.
