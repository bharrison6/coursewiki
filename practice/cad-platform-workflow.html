Revealing hidden edges: You want an unshaded view that distinguishes concealed edges from visible edges. Which style fits?

  1. Shaded without edges.

  3. Hidden edges visible.

Choice 3. Hidden edges visible includes concealed boundaries. Hidden edges removed omits them; shading without edges does not provide those outlines.

Normal to a plane: A planar face is tilted relative to all standard model planes. Which direction gives a normal view of that face?

  1. Along a direction perpendicular to the face.

Choice 1. Normal means perpendicular to the selected plane. It need not match a standard front, top, or side direction.

Section view or cut feature: You turn on a section view in the model window, inspect a cavity, then turn the section off. What changed?

Choice 2. The viewing section exposes the inside visually. It is not a material-removal feature or an automatically created drawing view.

Checking a mass result: A uniform solid has volume 12 cm³ and assigned density 2.5 g/cm³. What mass should the model report?

  1. 4.8 g.
  2. 14.5 g.
  3. 30 g.
Enter a number, then check it.
Show answer and reasoning

Choice 3. Mass = density × volume = 2.5 × 12 = 30 g. Dividing volume by density or adding the values gives the other choices, not mass.

Interpreting a measurement: Measure reports the minimum gap between two cylindrical faces, but you need the distance between their axes. What should you do?

Choice 2. Minimum surface gap and axis-to-axis distance answer different questions. Read the measurement label and check the selection.

Version or branch: You need a fixed milestone and a separate place to explore a new design. Which Onshape workflow fits?

  1. Create a version, then branch from it to an editable workspace.

Choice 1. A version preserves a design state. Branching from it creates a separate workspace for further changes.

Choice 3. The current sketch can contain today's edits even near the start of the feature sequence. Use history or a preserved earlier state to inspect older geometry.
