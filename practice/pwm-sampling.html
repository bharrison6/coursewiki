Separate point samples from the cycle mean

Phase-locked PWM sample. An ideal 0–5 V PWM waveform is HIGH for normalized phase [0, 0.25) and LOW for [0.25, 1). A point sampler runs once per PWM period, always at phase 0.10. What does every sample read? 1 = 5 V; 2 = 1.25 V; 3 = 0 V; 4 = it alternates.

1. Phase 0.10 lies inside the HIGH interval, so every point sample is 5 V. The complete-cycle time mean is still 25% × 5 V = 1.25 V; the sampler is not averaging a cycle.

LOW-phase finite sample mean. An ideal 25%, 0–5 V waveform is HIGH on normalized phase [0, 0.25) and LOW on [0.25, 1). It is sampled once per PWM period at phase 0.60 for 20 point samples. What is the mean of those selected samples?

Phase 0.60 is LOW on every cycle, so all 20 samples are 0 V and their mean is 0 V. A larger count does not remove phase locking.

Four-phase PWM sample mean. An ideal 25%, 0–5 V waveform is HIGH on normalized phase [0, 0.25) and LOW on [0.25, 1). One repeating four-sample lattice lands at phases 0.01, 0.26, 0.51 and 0.76. What is the analog mean of those four point values?

Only phase 0.01 is HIGH: (5 + 0 + 0 + 0)/4 = 1.25 V. This chosen lattice matches the complete-cycle time mean; faster sampling by itself does not guarantee that result.

Keep quantization and RC ripple distinct

Mean of ADC lower edges. In the ideal 10-bit, 5.000 V bin model, the four raw samples 5, 0, 0 and 0 V convert to codes 1023, 0, 0 and 0. Code 1023 reconstructs to lower edge 4.9951171875 V. What is the mean of the four reconstructed lower edges?

(4.9951171875 + 0 + 0 + 0)/4 = 1.248779296875 V. That is slightly below the 1.25 V analog sample mean because each code represents its bin's lower edge.

Settled RC ripple below the mean. The lesson's ideal unloaded RC model uses 25% duty, 500 Hz and τ = 10 ms. Its complete-cycle mean is 1.25 V; after settling, the phase-0 point is 1.157869414 V. How far below the cycle mean is that point, to four decimal places?

1.25 − 1.157869414 = 0.092130586 V, or 0.0921 V. The phase-0 sample is instantaneous settled ripple, not the cycle mean, and this model does not simulate startup.
