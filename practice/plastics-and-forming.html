Monomers and polymers: Which statement correctly connects monomers and polymers?

  1. A monomer is a finished plastic part.

  3. Polymer means any small metal particle.

Choice 2. Small starting molecules react to build the much larger polymer molecules. Polymer chains may also branch or become chemically linked.

Reheating cured epoxy: A fully cured epoxy casting must be reshaped. Which prediction fits its material type?

Choice 2. Cured epoxy is a thermoset. Its chemically linked network does not become a reusable melt. Heating is not a way to return it to the uncured liquid.

A dimple opposite a thick rib: An injection-molded cover has a shallow depression on the visible face directly opposite a heavy rib. Which design change should be investigated first?

  1. Make every rib as thick as possible.
  2. Reduce the heavy rib or core out the local mass.
  3. Remove all draft so the part stays in place.
Enter a number, then check it.
Show answer and reasoning

Choice 2. The thick region can shrink differently and pull the surface inward, producing sink. Reduce the local mass and check packing and cooling; filling every region thicker is not a general cure.
