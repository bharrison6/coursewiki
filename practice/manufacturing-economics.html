Manufacturing Economics Practice

Equal process costs. For a batch of acceptable parts, A costs $180 fixed plus $9/part; B costs $930 fixed plus $4/part. Both meet the specification. At what quantity are total costs equal? Give a whole number.

Enter an answer, then check it.
Show worked solution

180 + 9Q = 930 + 4Q; 5Q = 750; Q = 150 parts. Each total is $1,530. Dividing the fixed-cost difference by a sum of variable costs would not equate the totals.

Average unit cost. A batch has a $350 setup and $7.375 variable cost per acceptable part. All 80 parts are acceptable. Find average unit cost to the nearest cent.

Show worked solution

C = 350 + 7.375 × 80 = $940. Unit cost = 940/80 = $11.75/part. The $7.375 variable charge alone omits setup.

Share of a purchased sheet. A $60 sheet yields eight identical acceptable blanks. Charge the whole sheet, including waste, equally to the eight blanks. No other costs or remnant credits apply. Find material cost per blank to the nearest cent.

Enter an answer, then check it.
Show worked solution

Each blank is charged 1/8 of the sheet: (1/8) × $60 = $7.50. Do not charge the whole $60 to each blank.

Count blanks with cutting allowance. A 1,000 mm bar supplies 100 mm blanks. Each blank needs one 2 mm separating cut, including the last blank. Assume no end trim or other allowances. What is the maximum whole number of blanks?

Enter an answer, then check it.
Show worked solution

Each blank consumes 102 mm. Nine consume 918 mm; ten need 1,020 mm and will not fit. Answer: 9 blanks. There are 82 mm left over, plus 18 mm lost to nine kerfs. Ignoring kerf incorrectly predicts ten blanks.

Useful length as a percentage. Use a 1,020 mm bar to make nine acceptable 110 mm blanks of the same cross-section. Include all purchased stock in the denominator. What is utilization to one decimal place?

Enter an answer, then check it.
Show worked solution

Useful length = 9 × 110 = 990 mm. Utilization = 990/1,020 × 100 = 97.0588…%, or 97.1%. 1,020 − 9 × 110 = 30 mm of the bar lies outside the finished blanks (kerf plus offcut together).

Material BOM roll-up. One assembly needs three plates at $4.80 each and six fasteners at $0.30 each. Those prices already include material waste. Find the material-only BOM total to the nearest cent.

Enter an answer, then check it.
Show worked solution

3 × $4.80 + 6 × $0.30 = $14.40 + $1.80 = $16.20. Processing and assembly costs are not included.
