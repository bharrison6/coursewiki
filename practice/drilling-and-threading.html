Plan holes and read threads

Estimate an inch cutting-tap drill. Estimate the cutting-tap drill for a 5/16-18 thread using major diameter minus 1/TPI. Give the mathematical estimate to four decimal places in inches, before choosing a stock drill.

Enter or choose an answer, then check it.
Show explanation

5/16 = 0.3125 in; pitch = 1/18 in. Subtracting gives 0.256944… in, or 0.2569 in. The number 18 is TPI, not a length. Starting with a minor diameter would subtract thread depth twice.

Estimate a metric cutting-tap drill. Estimate the cutting-tap drill for M10 × 1.5 using major diameter minus pitch. Give the mathematical estimate to two decimal places in millimeters.

Enter or choose an answer, then check it.
Show explanation

The nominal major diameter is 10 mm and pitch is 1.5 mm. 10 − 1.5 = 8.50 mm. Taking 1/1.5 treats a pitch as though it were TPI and gives the wrong dimension.

Reason about thread percentage. Two holes will receive the same cutting tap. Within the permitted hole-size range, what generally happens when the prepared hole diameter increases?

Enter or choose an answer, then check it.
Show explanation

Thread height decreases and tapping torque generally decreases. The larger hole leaves less material for the tap to cut. Engagement length is an axial distance and is a separate design choice; thread percentage is not a strength percentage.

Choose the correct tap-drill reference. A process changes from a cutting tap to a forming tap for the same thread. Which preparation is appropriate?

Enter or choose an answer, then check it.
Show explanation

Use the forming-tap guidance. Forming displaces material rather than removing chips and generally needs a larger starting hole. The cutting-tap approximation is not a forming-tap specification.

Read an internal Unified thread. Which reading of 3/8-16 UNC-2B is correct?

Enter or choose an answer, then check it.
Show explanation

0.375 in nominal major diameter, 16 TPI, Unified coarse, class 2 internal. B identifies an internal thread. The major diameter is not the tap-drill diameter, and thread class is not strength grade.

Read an external fine thread. Which reading of 3/8-24 UNF-3A is correct?

Enter or choose an answer, then check it.
Show explanation

0.375 in nominal major diameter, 24 TPI, Unified fine, class 3 external. A identifies the external thread. The class concerns dimensional fit; it is not the fastener material grade.

Read a metric thread pitch. What does M8 × 1.25 tell you?

Enter or choose an answer, then check it.
Show explanation

Metric thread, 8 mm nominal major diameter and 1.25 mm pitch. The callout shown does not state a tolerance class. The pitch is a spacing, not a count per inch.

Convert TPI to pitch. A single-start thread has 20 threads per inch. What is its axial pitch? Give inches to three decimal places.

Enter or choose an answer, then check it.
Show explanation

Pitch = 1/20 = 0.050 in. Because this example is single-start, its lead is also 0.050 in per revolution. TPI itself is not a travel distance.

Recess a cylindrical screw head. The drawing calls for a cylindrical recess with a flat shoulder around a smaller through hole, so a screw head can sit below the surface. Which operation matches?

Enter or choose an answer, then check it.
Show explanation

Counterbore. Its cylindrical recess and flat shoulder provide the required head space. A countersink would create a cone.

Finish a nearly sized pin hole. A hole is on location and has the recommended small finishing allowance. The goal is its final diameter and improved surface finish for a pin. Which operation best matches this plan?

Enter or choose an answer, then check it.
Show explanation

Ream. A reamer removes a small allowance to improve size and finish. It should not be expected to relocate a badly misplaced hole.

Make a local bearing seat. A rough surface around a hole needs a shallow flat area for a washer to bear on. No deep head recess is required. Which operation best describes the purpose?

Enter or choose an answer, then check it.
Show explanation

Spot face. It creates a local flat bearing surface. A counterbore may use similar tooling but serves a specified recess.

Correct an existing hole axis. An existing hole needs its axis corrected relative to the machine setup. Enough material remains for the intended corrected bore to clean up. Which operation offers control of axis and diameter?

Enter or choose an answer, then check it.
Show explanation

Bore. The boring tool path can be set from the machine axis. A reamer tends to follow the existing hole.

Make a conical screw seat. The specified flat-head screw needs a matching conical seat. Which operation makes that feature?

Enter or choose an answer, then check it.
Show explanation

Countersink. Select its angle and diameter from the specified head and drawing; the thread size alone does not specify the seat.
