Calculate solid round bar mass: A hypothetical stock estimate specifies density = 2,700 kg/m³. Use that supplied assumption, not a certified alloy property. A solid round bar has diameter 20 mm and length 500 mm. Find its mass in kg, rounded to three decimal places.

Enter a number, then check it.
Show answer and reasoning

Radius = 0.010 m and length = 0.500 m. Volume = π × 0.010² × 0.500 = 0.000157080 m³. Mass = volume × 2,700 = 0.424115 kg, or 0.424 kg. Using the diameter as the radius gives 1.69646 kg, four times too much. This is mass, not weight force.

Calculate tube material volume: Round tube has outside diameter 30 mm, wall thickness 3 mm and length 200 mm. Find the volume of metal in cm³, rounded to two decimal places. Ignore end features and manufacturing tolerances.

Enter a number, then check it.

Inside diameter = 30 − 2 × 3 = 24 mm. Volume = π(30² − 24²) × 200 / 4 = 50,893.801 mm³. Divide by 1,000 to obtain 50.89 cm³. Treating the tube as solid gives 141.37 cm³; subtracting only one wall gives 26.86 cm³.
