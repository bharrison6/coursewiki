Assume the tools have adequate range and are in good condition.

Enter a number, then check it.

Total tolerance = 0.001 in; target increment = 0.0001 in. Choose 3, the outside micrometer. Its accuracy and method still need verification; this resolution rule is not an acceptance standard.

26° + 7 × 5′ = 26°35′. In total minutes: 26 × 60 + 35 = 1595 arcmin. Read the vernier in the same direction as the main scale.

Obtuse angle from an acute supplement. The contact arrangement measures the acute supplement of the required obtuse angle. The scale shows 41 completed degrees and vernier division 9 aligns on a 5-minute instrument. Report the REQUIRED obtuse angle in total arcminutes.

Enter a number, then check it.
Show worked solution

Scale = 41°45′ = 2505 arcmin. Required angle = 180° − 41°45′ = 138°15′. Thus 10800 − 2505 = 8295 arcmin. Subtract only because the problem explicitly identifies the measured supplement.
