Line loss at the lower voltage. An ideal DC comparison transmits 6,000 W at the sending end at 300 V through a total line resistance of 0.8 Ω. What is the line heating loss? Give one decimal place.

I = 6000 / 300 = 20 A; loss = 20² × 0.8 = 320.0 W. The line resistance includes the complete current path.

Both paths remain connected. The meter reads their equivalent resistance: (68 × 120) / (68 + 120) = 43.404255… Ω, or 43.40 Ω, not the resistance of either part alone.
