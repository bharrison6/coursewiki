Read sections and derived views

Identify a straight complete cut. Which section type uses the straight cut through the entire cylinder shown here?

[[img:engineering-graphics/section-views/full-section.svg|Straight cut across a hollow cylinder and the resulting pair of hatched walls.]]

Choose an answer, then check it.
Show explanation

Full. One straight plane passes through the whole part. Both walls are sectioned.

Identify a quarter removal. Which section type produces this combined exterior and interior view?

[[img:engineering-graphics/section-views/half-section.svg|Two perpendicular cutting-plane portions and a result with exterior left and section right.]]

Choose an answer, then check it.
Show explanation

Half. Removing one quarter of a symmetric part produces a view with half exterior and half section.

Identify a jogged cut. Which section type follows the jogged path through all three holes?

[[img:engineering-graphics/section-views/offset-section.svg|A cutting path jogs through three staggered holes; its result shows all three openings.]]

Choose an answer, then check it.
Show explanation

Offset. The parallel cutting portions are offset to reach staggered holes. The jogs are not edges on the manufactured plate.

Identify a local cutaway. Which section type exposes this passage within an irregular boundary?

[[img:engineering-graphics/section-views/broken-out-section.svg|An irregular local boundary exposes a passage in a housing.]]

Choose an answer, then check it.
Show explanation

Broken-out. A broken-out section removes only a local area to a selected depth in the existing view.

Identify an overlaid slice. The rectangular slice is turned into the drawing plane and placed on the parent bar. What is it?

[[img:engineering-graphics/section-views/revolved-section.svg|A rectangular cross section is overlaid at the cutting location on a bar.]]

Choose an answer, then check it.
Show explanation

Revolved section. A revolved section places the rotated cross section on the parent view.

Identify a separate slice. The slice is shown clear of the parent bar and identified by its cut letters. What is it?

[[img:engineering-graphics/section-views/removed-section.svg|A bar has a transverse A–A cut and a separate rectangular section.]]

Choose an answer, then check it.
Show explanation

Removed section. A removed section is placed apart from the parent view and linked to its location by identification.

Identify a rotated radial cut. One radial cutting portion is rotated into the other before drawing the result. Which section type is used?

[[img:engineering-graphics/section-views/aligned-section.svg|Two intersecting radial cutting planes through a disk, with an arc showing rotation into alignment.]]

Choose an answer, then check it.
Show explanation

Aligned. An aligned section rotates an angled cutting portion into alignment; the top view retains the actual angular locations.

Choose the rib hatch convention. A section plane passes lengthwise through a thin reinforcing rib. Which treatment usually avoids making the rib look like a solid wall?

Choose an answer, then check it.
Show explanation

Leave the lengthwise rib unhatched. A rib cut along its length is conventionally left unhatched; a transverse cut through its thickness is hatched.

Distinguish shaft cut directions. Which statement is appropriate for a solid shaft in an assembly drawing?

Choose an answer, then check it.
Show explanation

A longitudinal shaft is commonly unsectioned; a transverse section can be hatched. The convention depends on cut direction and context. It does not make all shafts immune to sectioning.

Match a cutting plane to its result. Follow the X–X arrows. Which candidate matches the section through this plate?

[[img:engineering-graphics/section-views/match-cut.svg|A plate has one hole on cut X–X and another below it; arrows look upward. Candidate A has one opening and candidate B has two.]]

Choose an answer, then check it.
Show explanation

Result A. Result A. The straight plane crosses the left hole. The right hole lies in the portion removed toward the viewer, so it does not appear in this section.

Interpret the offset jog. The cutting plane jogs across solid material between two holes. Should that jog appear as a new physical edge in the resulting section?

Choose an answer, then check it.
Show explanation

No, the jog changes the imaginary cut path. The offset cutting path is a drawing device, not a step machined into the part.

Choose a true-shape view. An inclined face appears as a sloping line in the front view. Which view shows the complete face in true shape?

Choose an answer, then check it.
Show explanation

An auxiliary view looking perpendicular to that face. The projection plane must be parallel to the face. Enlargement does not remove foreshortening.

Choose the projector direction. For a primary auxiliary construction, the fold line is parallel to the edge view of the inclined face. How do you draw the projectors?

Choose an answer, then check it.
Show explanation

Perpendicular to the fold line. Projectors are normal to the auxiliary projection plane as represented by its fold line; their direction follows the chosen face.

Read a partial auxiliary. A partial auxiliary shows only the sloping face and nearby identifying geometry. What has changed from a complete auxiliary?

Choose an answer, then check it.
Show explanation

The coverage is limited; the projection direction is unchanged. Partial describes which region is shown, not a new projection system.

Read a detail scale. A detail is marked 2:1. What does that tell you?

Choose an answer, then check it.
Show explanation

The drawn lengths are twice actual lengths at the intended print scale. A 2:1 view is an enlargement. The actual feature size remains the stated dimension; resized screens or prints are not measuring tools.

Read a broken view. A continuous rod is drawn with two break lines and an overall dimension of 360 mm. What is its specified overall length?

Choose an answer, then check it.
Show explanation

360 mm. The dimension gives the full length. The break saves sheet space and does not remove length from the manufactured rod.
