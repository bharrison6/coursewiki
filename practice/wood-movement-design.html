Center-fixed edge travel

Center-fixed edge travel. A flat-sawn white fir board is 9.15 in wide when installed at 8% MC and reaches 11% MC. Use CT = 0.00245 per percentage point MC. Assume uniform free width change on a stationary support, with the center as the only fixed reference. How far does the right edge move outward? Give inches to four decimal places.

Enter a number, then check it.
Show answer and reasoning

Total width change = 9.15 × 0.00245 × (11 − 8) = 0.0672525 in. The right edge is half a width from the reference, so it moves 0.0672525 ÷ 2 = 0.0336 in outward. The total width change is shared by two edges.

Left-edge-fixed travel

Left-edge-fixed travel. A flat-sawn white fir board is installed 9.15 in wide at 8% MC. It reaches 11% MC; CT = 0.00245 per percentage point MC. Assume uniform free width change and a stationary support with the left edge as the only fixed reference. How far does the right edge move outward? Give inches to four decimal places.

Drying reverses travel

Drying reverses travel. A flat-sawn white fir board is installed 9.15 in wide at 8% MC, then dries to 6% MC. Use CT = 0.00245 and assume uniform free width change with only the center fixed to a stationary support. Find signed right-edge travel: positive means outward, negative means inward toward the fixed reference. Give inches to four decimal places, including the sign.

Enter a number, then check it.
Show answer and reasoning

ΔW = 9.15 × 0.00245 × (6 − 8) = −0.044835 in. Right-edge travel = ΔW ÷ 2 = −0.0224 in. This uses the inward allowance, even if ample outward allowance exists.

A point inside the edge

A point inside the edge. A 9.15 in wide board has a calculated free width increase of 0.0672525 in. Assume uniform strain and a stationary support with only the center fixed. A tracking point is one quarter of the installed full width to the right of the center. How far does this point move outward? Give inches to four decimal places.

Enter a number, then check it.
Show answer and reasoning

Point travel = (distance from reference ÷ installed width) × ΔW = 0.25 × 0.0672525 = 0.0168 in outward. The edge is farther from the reference and travels 0.0336 in; this point is halfway to that edge.
