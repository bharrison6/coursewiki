Cure selected regions in a vat

A build platform sits with liquid photopolymer. Light exposes selected regions for each layer, and the part later needs the specified cleaning and post-cure. Which family is described?

Choice 1. The feedstock is liquid photopolymer in a vat and light selectively cures it. SLA and DLP are implementations with different exposure methods.

Distinguish binder from fusion

A layer of metal powder is spread. A printhead deposits liquid only where a fragile green shape should form. The printed shape will later follow its specified debinding and sintering route. Which family is described?

Choose an answer, then check it.
Show reasoning

Choice 3. The powder is the build material and the jetted liquid is binder. The green shape is an intermediate state; later consolidation is part of this stated metal route.

Follow the jetted material

A printhead deposits photopolymer droplets that become the part after UV curing. A second deposited material supports the part and is removed later. What distinguishes this material-jetting example from binder jetting?

Choose an answer, then check it.
Show reasoning

Choice 1. In this example the jetted photopolymer is build material. PolyJet is a Stratasys example within material jetting, not the generic family name.

Classify an agent-assisted powder route

Polymer powder is spread. A printhead places fusing and detailing agents, then heat fuses selected regions. Loose powder surrounds the parts. Which family contains this Multi Jet Fusion example?

Choose an answer, then check it.
Show reasoning

Choice 2. The joining mechanism is thermal fusion of powder. A printhead alone does not determine the family; follow what the agent does to the build material.

Decide whether printing is the last step

A specified metal binder-jet route ends the machine cycle with a depowdered green part. The process plan calls for debinding, sintering, dimensional inspection and machining of one mating face. Which statement is accurate?

Choose an answer, then check it.
Show reasoning

Choice 3. A green shape is not automatically the final dense metal part. The required route continues through its specified post-build steps, including inspection after dimensional change.
