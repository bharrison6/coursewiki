A = 30 × (5 − 4) ÷ (8 − 5) = 10.00 L. Final volume is 40 L, below the 45 L limit. Concentrate balance: (30 × 4 + 10 × 8) ÷ 40 = 5%.

Concentrate = 16 × 10.25 ÷ 100 = 1.64 L. Water = 16 − 1.64 = 14.36 L. Put water in first, then add concentrate while mixing.
