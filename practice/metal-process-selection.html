Casting size versus shrinkage voids

Which feature supplies liquid metal to a casting as it solidifies?

  1. Draft
  2. The core
  3. A properly designed riser
Enter a choice number, then check it.
Show answer and reasoning

Choice 3. The riser feeds contraction during solidification. Draft aids release and the core creates an internal opening; neither replaces feeding.

Join repeated overlapping sheets

Many overlapping steel-sheet assemblies need discrete permanent joints. Both sides are accessible. Which process fits these conditions?

  1. Resistance spot welding
  2. Soldering simply because it uses less heat
  3. Extrusion
Enter a choice number, then check it.

Choice 1. Resistance spot welding forms local nuggets at overlapping sheets and suits repeated production. Separate spots are not a continuous leak-tight seam.

Join without melting the base parts

  1. Fusion welding only
  2. Rolling
  3. Brazing or soldering

Choice 3. Brazing and soldering melt filler rather than the base parts. Filler temperature regime, compatibility and service requirements distinguish the choice.

Spot the galvanic risk

Choice 1. The first arrangement supplies both electrolyte and electrical connection. Dissimilar names alone do not complete a cell.

Decode an inch thread

What does the thread-size designation 1/4-20 UNC communicate?

  1. Nominal 1/4-inch thread diameter, 20 threads per inch, Unified coarse series
  2. 1/4-inch bolt length and a 20-inch pitch
  3. 6 mm nominal diameter and 1 mm pitch
Choose a number, then check it.
Show answer and reasoning

Choice 1. The first value is nominal diameter; 20 is a count of threads over one inch, so pitch is 1/20 inch. UNC names the Unified coarse series. Bolt length and the remaining specification are separate.

Decode a metric thread

What does M6 × 1 mean, and does it match 1/4-20 UNC?

  1. 6 mm wrench size and one thread per inch; it matches
  2. 6 mm bolt length and 1 mm diameter; it matches
  3. 6 mm nominal thread diameter and 1 mm pitch; it does not match
Choose a number, then check it.
Show answer and reasoning

Choice 3. M6 identifies nominal 6 mm thread diameter; 1 is the axial spacing in millimeters. An inch designation uses threads per inch instead. These two sizes differ in diameter and spacing; neither designation specifies wrench size or bolt length.

Classify the filler temperature

A close-fitting joint is filled by capillary action. The filler has a liquidus of 600°C and both base parts remain solid. Which process matches?

  1. Soldering because the base parts stay solid
  2. Brazing because the filler liquidus is above 450°C
  3. Fusion welding because some metal becomes liquid
Choose a number, then check it.
Show answer and reasoning

Choice 2. The filler liquidus, not the flame temperature, is compared with 450°C. At 600°C it is in the brazing regime. Both brazing and soldering keep the base parts solid; fusion welding melts base metal at the joint.

Compare wetted galvanic areas

Compare the same active/noble metal pair in the same electrolyte with electrical contact. Which arrangement can concentrate galvanic attack on the active metal most strongly?

  1. A large wetted active area coupled to a small wetted noble area
  2. A dry, fully isolated pair
  3. A small wetted active area coupled to a large wetted noble area
Choose a number, then check it.
Show answer and reasoning

Choice 3. The small active anode receives the attack associated with the larger wetted cathode. A coating defect can create that unfavorable area ratio. The dry isolated pair lacks the conditions stated for the working cell; area ratio alone does not predict service life.
