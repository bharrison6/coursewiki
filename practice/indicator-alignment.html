The midpoint raw reading is +0.030 mm. Zeroing subtracts that value from every position, so the left becomes 0.000 − 0.030 = −0.030 mm. The right becomes +0.030 mm. Right minus left is still +0.060 mm.

Choice 2. Equal endpoints describe only two positions. A center departure is not the smooth linear trend corrected by moving one end. Inspect the local surface and setup, then repeat the full sweep.
