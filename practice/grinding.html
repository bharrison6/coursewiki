Count full-face downfeed increments: An idealized plan removes 0.018 in from one face at 0.0015 in per full-face layer. How many downfeed increments are needed? 0.018 / 0.0015 = 12 increments. The units of length cancel. This is not a count of individual table strokes.

Plan a smaller final increment: An idealized plan must remove 0.017 in from one face. Eight full-face layers have each removed exactly 0.002 in. What thickness remains for the final layer? Give inches to three decimal places. Ignore wheel wear and elastic effects; these are arithmetic values, not settings.

Enter a number, then check it.
Show worked solution

0.017 − (8 × 0.002) = 0.001 in. A ninth full 0.002 in layer would remove too much. Use the smaller remainder in this idealized plan.
