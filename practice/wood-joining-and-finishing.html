Choice 3. Clips can hold the top while allowing travel. The movement direction and clearance must match the design; a finish does not remove that need.

Choice 2. Investigate the epoxy system and its gap limit, substrate preparation and cure instructions. A structural bonding filler differs from an easily sanded fairing filler. Foam expansion alone is not evidence of strength.

Unclamping versus loading: A product’s minimum clamp interval has elapsed, but its stated interval before loading has not. What follows from those instructions?

  1. The joint is ready for its full service load
  2. The glue must have failed
  3. Removing restraint and applying service loads are different milestones

Show explanation

Choice 3. Clamp time and cure/loading time are separate. Follow both instructions under the specified conditions rather than assuming that an unclamped joint has full cured properties.

Order the preparation grits: A sample calls for all four grits: 80, 120, 150 and 180. Which sequence progressively removes coarser scratches?

  1. 180 → 150 → 120 → 80
  2. 80 → 120 → 150 → 180
  3. 80 → 180 → 120 → 150
Enter a number, then check it.
Show explanation

Choice 2. Use 80 → 120 → 150 → 180, inspecting for removal of the previous scratches at each step. This exercise specifies the grits; a real finishing system may call for a different start or endpoint.

Finish for frequent wiping: A surface will receive frequent wiping and light abrasion. The owner wants visible grain and accepts a film appearance. Which is the best starting candidate?

  1. A clear film finish explicitly rated for that service
  2. Pigment stain alone
  3. Wax alone chosen for maximum wear resistance
Enter a number, then check it.
Show explanation

Choice 1. A suitable clear film can provide wear and spill protection while leaving grain visible. Check the exact formulation and complete preparation/cure schedule. Stain primarily supplies color; wax alone provides limited protection.

A level open-pore surface: A sample has open pores but no dents. A level high-gloss surface is wanted. Which preparation product directly addresses those pores?

  1. A screw-hole plug
  2. Pre-stain conditioner alone
  3. A compatible grain/pore filler
Enter a number, then check it.
Show explanation

Choice 3. Pore filler levels the open texture. Conditioner addresses uneven stain absorption; it does not perform the same job. Test filler order and color in the complete finishing system.
