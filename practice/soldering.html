Name an inline wrapped splice. Each free wire end wraps around the opposite standing wire, making an inline connection. Name it. Enter the choice number.

3. Western Union splice. Western Union uses opposing wraps. A lap overlaps straight ends; a pigtail twists ends together on the same side.
