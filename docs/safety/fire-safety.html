Fire safety is a universal rule and belongs in this module. There is no source text for it.

The “Fire Safety” slide in General Safety.pptx (slide 15) carries two images and no text at all — whatever was said about it was said out loud. So there is nothing to carry across, and this page has to be authored from scratch rather than converted.

It is deliberately excluded from the General Shop Safety deck until it has content. A projected placeholder is worse than an acknowledged gap.

What it needs to cover

What it needs to cover

A starting outline, for the instructor to confirm or replace — none of this is established policy yet:

  • Extinguisher classes present in each lab, and which fire each one is for.
  • The decision rule: when to use an extinguisher, and when to evacuate instead.
  • Extinguisher and alarm pull-station locations, per lab.
  • Evacuation routes and the assembly point.
  • Hot-work rules — welding, grinding, torch — and any permit or watch requirement.
  • Flammable storage, which currently lives in chemical safety.
