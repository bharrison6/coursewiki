DET/Shop Safety

Shop Safety

The rules that apply in every DET lab, every day - what to wear, what to check, who has to be present, and what to do when something goes wrong.

Universal Rules

Chemicals and Safety Data Sheets Every chemical and liquid in the shop has a Safety Data Sheet. Read it before you use the material, not after. Electrical Safety Voltages present in the lab, the inspection required before every use, and what to do when someone is shocked. Equipment Operating Procedure The procedure that applies to every piece of equipment in every lab, every time you use it. Fire Safety Extinguisher classes, when to fight a fire and when to leave. Not yet written. Lab Conduct and Emergencies What is never acceptable in a lab, what alertness actually requires, and what to do when something goes wrong. Lifting and Carrying Team lifting above 40 lbs, and the technique that prevents the injury. Most back injuries come from poor technique, not from heavy loads. Machine Hazards and Housekeeping Rotating machinery, the environment around it, and the operating habits that keep both from hurting you. Personal Protective Equipment What you wear, and when. Eye protection is mandatory in the woods and metals lab at all times; the rest depends on the task.

Equipment

Equipment Categories and Supervision Every machine in every lab falls into one of three categories. The category tells you who has to be standing there before you switch it on.
