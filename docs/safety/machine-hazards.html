Machine Hazards and Housekeeping

Rotating machinery, the environment around it, and the operating habits that keep both from hurting you.

Rotating machinery

Rotating machinery

Gears, pulleys and belts, chains and sprockets are extremely dangerous pinch points.

  • NEVER work on equipment with these features without first disconnecting power.
  • Lockout/Tagout procedures must be followed for maintenance.

Loose clothing, jewelry and unsecured hair are what these features catch — see clothing, jewelry and hair.

The environment around the machine

The environment around the machine

Slip hazards
Clean dust, metal chips, water and coolant off the floor immediately.
Lighting
Ensure lighting is adequate before operating any machine.
Walkways
Keep aisles and emergency exits unobstructed.
Operating habits

Operating habits

  • Stop machines completely before taking measurements or making adjustments.
  • Secure all workpieces before operation — use clamps, vises or fixtures.
  • Never leave tools on or near operating machinery.
  • Use proper tools — gloves or rags when handling sharp objects such as cutters.

The shutdown and cleaning steps that close every session are in After operating any equipment.
