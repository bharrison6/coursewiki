Engineering Fundamentals

Shared mathematical and technical concepts for describing, calculating, and communicating engineering work.

Units and Measurement

Acceleration and Squared Time Calculate change in velocity over time, distinguish average from instantaneous acceleration, and convert squared time units correctly. Dimensional Analysis Convert quantities by multiplying by named equalities, cancelling units, and treating squared and cubed units as powers. Geometry, Mass, and Force Scale CAD geometry correctly, connect volume and density to mass, and keep force and weight as separate quantities. Measurement Language Use quantities, units, symbols, and SI prefixes precisely before a drawing, program, or calculation has a chance to drift. Motion Quantities Distinguish path length from displacement, and speed from velocity, before using time to describe a machine or vehicle in motion.
