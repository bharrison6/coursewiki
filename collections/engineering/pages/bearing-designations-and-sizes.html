Read the basic designation before the extra letters

A bearing number is a compact description, not a universal sizing formula. Start with the maker and the bearing family. A basic designation identifies a series and bore; prefixes and suffixes add details. The same letters can mean different things in different families or manufacturers' catalogs.

For the ordinary metric deep-groove example 6205, read 6 | (0)2 | 05: type 6 is deep-groove ball; 02 is the dimension series, with width-series 0 omitted in the written number; bore code 05 means 25 mm. The diameter-series digit 2 is not an outside diameter in millimetres.

Width-series digits can be omitted differently: NSK lists 6005 as 6 | (1)0 | 05, and 6305 as 6 | (0)3 | 05. Parentheses here explain the omitted digit; they are not characters to add to an order. See NSK's bearing-series table.

Recognize the geometry first in [[bearing-types-and-loads|Bearing Types and Loads]]. A matching bore alone does not make two bearings interchangeable.

Thus 6202 has a 15 mm bore, not 10 mm; 6205 has a 25 mm bore. This rule supplies only the bore. It does not calculate D or B.

Do not apply it blindly to miniature, inch, letter-coded or special bearings. Slash forms can state the bore directly: NSK gives 63/22 as 22 mm and 230/560 as 560 mm. Its miniature 688 example has an 8 mm bore. NSK excludes double-direction thrust ball bearings from its general 04–96 rule.

A dimension series selects a standardized size family; it is a table key, not a digit-to-millimetres formula. Series examples follow NSK's designation guide. Different types also use different dimension letters. A tapered bearing distinguishes inner-ring width B, outer-ring width C and assembled width T. Thrust catalogs may label axial height T or H: use that manufacturer's diagram rather than substituting B automatically.

C3 means internal clearance, not accuracy or a loose shaft fit

Internal clearance is movement between a bearing's rings under the specified measuring conditions. A fit is the relationship between a ring and its shaft or housing seat. A precision class specifies dimensional and running accuracy. These are different quantities; see NSK's explanation of fits and clearance.

Prefixes matter too: NSK uses HR for its high-capacity tapered roller series. Read the complete maker-specific designation, including cage and lubrication details where required. Seals and shields affect exclusion of contaminants, friction and speed limits; they do not replace the catalog's load ratings.
