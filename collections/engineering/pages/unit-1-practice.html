Method: start with the given quantity, multiply by equal-quantity ratios, and stop only when the requested unit remains. For example:

20 m × 1 ft0.3048 m = 65.6168 ft. The crossed-out meter shows why the factor faces this way.

Keep guard digits while calculating. Round only the final reported value, and state the unit the problem asks for.

Line rates and count rates

These problems combine a spacing or a count with a travel rate. Treat part, word, and key as countable units that can cancel.

1. Assembly-line travel time: Two stations are 10 m apart. Parts enter a conveyor 1 ft apart, and the line processes 2 parts every 15 s at a steady rate. How long does one part take to travel from the first station to the second?

Enter a number, then check it.
Show worked solution

The spacing supplies 1 part / 1 ft, and the throughput supplies 15 s / 2 parts. Therefore 10 m × (1 ft / 0.3048 m) × (1 part / 1 ft) × (15 s / 2 parts) = 246.063 s, or 246 s (about 4.10 min). This assumes constant belt speed and steady spacing.

2. Recycling-line loading: A recycling conveyor carries 72 lb of material per linear foot. It moves continuously at 0.23 m/s. How many lb of material pass a point in one hour?

Enter a number, then check it.
Show worked solution

Carry the line loading, speed, exact foot factor, and hour conversion together: 0.23 m/s × (1 ft / 0.3048 m) × (72 lb / ft) × (3,600 s / 1 h) = 195,590.551… lb/h. The result is 1.96 × 105 lb/h (about 195,600 lb/h).

3. Typing count rate: A typist produces 150 words/min. The average word has 5.1 letters, and one spacebar press follows each word, for 6.1 key presses/word. What is the typist’s key rate in keys/s?

Enter a number, then check it.
Show worked solution

150 words/min × (6.1 keys / 1 word) × (1 min / 60 s) = 15.25 keys/s. Words and minutes cancel, leaving 15.25 keys/s.

Speed and exact-length conversions

Speed is a nonnegative magnitude. If a problem names a positive direction, a negative signed result would instead describe a velocity component opposite that direction.

4. Average running speed: A runner completes 5 km in 12 min 37 s. What is the runner’s average speed in mi/h?

Enter a number, then check it.
Show worked solution

First convert the distance and time: 5 km × (1 mi / 1.609344 km) = 3.10685596 mi and 12 min 37 s = 757 s. Then 3.10685596 mi / 757 s × (3,600 s / 1 h) = 14.775… mi/h, or 14.8 mph.

5. Conveyor setting: A packaging conveyor must cover 20 m in 12 s. What speed setting is required in ft/s?

Enter a number, then check it.
Show worked solution

20 m / 12 s × (1 ft / 0.3048 m) = 5.468066… ft/s. The required speed is 5.47 ft/s.

6. Reverse exact-factor check: A guard is specified as 18 ft long. What is its length in meters?

Enter a number, then check it.
Show worked solution

Put feet on the bottom so they cancel: 18 ft × (0.3048 m / 1 ft) = 5.4864 m. The exact relationship gives 5.4864 m.

Prefixes, area, and volume powers

A prefix changes a scale by a power of ten. Squared and cubed units require the whole linear conversion factor to be squared or cubed.

7. Prefix scale: A feature is 0.0048 m long. What is its length in mm?

Enter a number, then check it.
Show worked solution

0.0048 m × (1,000 mm / 1 m) = 4.8 mm. The answer is 4.8 mm.

8. Squared conversion: A panel has an area of 2.75 ft2. What is its area in in2?

Enter a number, then check it.
Show worked solution

Because 1 ft = 12 in, square the relationship: 2.75 ft2 × (12 in / 1 ft)2 = 2.75 × 144 in2 = 396 in2. The answer is 396 in².

9. Cubed conversion: A fixture has a volume of 0.50 ft3. What is its volume in in3?

Enter a number, then check it.
Show worked solution

Cube the 12-in-per-foot relationship: 0.50 ft3 × (12 in / 1 ft)3 = 0.50 × 1,728 in3 = 864 in3. The answer is 864 in³.

Signed velocity and acceleration

Choose the positive direction before calculating. A negative result is meaningful: it says the velocity component or acceleration points opposite that chosen direction.

10. Conveyor acceleration: A conveyor accelerates from rest (0 m/s) to 3 m/s in 5 s. What is its average acceleration?

Enter a number, then check it.
Show worked solution

aavg = (vf − vi) / Δt = (3 m/s − 0 m/s) / 5 s = 0.6 m/s2. The answer is +0.6 m/s² in the selected positive direction.

11. Direction reversal: Take motion to the right as positive. A cart changes from +2 m/s to −1 m/s in 0.75 s. What is its average acceleration?

Enter a number, then check it.
Show worked solution

aavg = (−1 m/s − (+2 m/s)) / 0.75 s = −3 m/s / 0.75 s = −4 m/s2. The negative sign means the acceleration component points left, opposite the chosen positive direction.

Production-cycle planning

Production planning combines a stated operating window with a repeatable cycle. A partial cycle is not a complete part.

12. Complete-part schedule: A cell runs for 7.5 h. Setup takes 20 min, and each repeatable cycle takes 3.6 min per part. How many complete parts fit in the run?

Enter a number, then check it.
Show worked solution

The running window is 7.5 h × (60 min / 1 h) − 20 min = 430 min. Then 430 min / (3.6 min / part) = 119.444… parts. Only 119 complete parts fit; the fraction is time into the next cycle.

Scope and assumptions

These are idealized calculation checks. They assume the stated spacing, speed, cycle time, and throughput remain constant; real production plans also account for startup, pauses, quality checks, material availability, and safety constraints.
