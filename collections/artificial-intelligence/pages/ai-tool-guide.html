The audience thinks “using AI” means a chat box. It is one surface out of four, and the least capable. A chat window, the agent already inside Docs or Word, a desktop coworker, a coding agent — set against 4 axes: what it can see, what it can do, whether it keeps going unwatched, and how much you must check. Then 7 real faculty jobs, and for each one the smallest door that finishes it and exactly what you hand over to get there.
