Explore Prompting Strategies

A question sits beside three toggleable levers, and the answer changes in exactly one dimension per lever. Sixty-four hand-written answers, each labelled as hand-written on the panel that shows it, a deck that decides your answer’s shape whenever you don’t, and a meter counting the expert vocabulary you got for free.

The activity runs in your browser without an AI service. Use its Guide button for instructions and Settings for presenter notes, presentation mode and reset. Open it full screen when you want more room.

Learning path

  • Context. What changes when it knows who is asking?
  • Success spec. If you don't say what finished looks like, who does?
  • Expert keywords. What do you get for asking in the field's words?
