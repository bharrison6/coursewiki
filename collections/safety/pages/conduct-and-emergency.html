Prohibited behaviour

  • NEVER operate equipment under the influence of drugs, alcohol, or medication that causes drowsiness.
  • NO horseplay, pranks or roughhousing in any lab area.
  • Do not distract others who are operating equipment.
  • No unauthorized experiments or modifications to equipment.

Staying alert

  • Stay focused — maintain concentration while operating equipment.
  • Take breaks when tired or distracted.
  • Ask questions if you are unsure about any procedure.
  • Report unsafe conditions immediately to the instructor.

Emergency procedures

  • Know the location of emergency stops, fire extinguishers, first aid kits and exits.
  • Call 911 for serious injuries or fires.
  • Notify the instructor immediately of any incident, injury, or near-miss.
  • Do not move a seriously injured person unless they are in immediate danger.

For electrical shock specifically, the first action is different — see [[electrical-safety|If someone is shocked]].

Workspace responsibility

  • Clean as you go — do not wait until the end.
  • Report equipment problems before they become safety hazards.
