Manual mill: Category 3; CNC mill: Category 2 in the Metals lab

A vertical mill uses a rotating cutter to remove material held in a vise or clamps. See [[vertical-mill-anatomy]] for the machine's parts.

The manual mill is Category 3 in the [[metals-lab-equipment|Metals lab]]; the CNC mill is Category 2 there. Use [[equipment-categories]] for the corresponding permission and supervision requirements.

Start with [[equipment-operating-procedure]], [[ppe]], [[machine-hazards]] and [[metal-chips]]. For the Tormach workflow, use [[mill-setup-checklist]]; its controller sequence is not a startup sequence for a manual mill.

Check clamping, cutter travel and cutting settings before the cut

In our labs, check and recheck the workpiece clamping before beginning an operation.

  • Check the vise or clamps and the cutter's retention. Confirm the tool can follow the planned path without striking the workholding.
  • Identify the closing gaps between the moving table, workholding and machine frame. Keep the planned travel clear.
  • Check spindle speed, feed, depth of cut and width of cut for the planned operation.
  • Check coolant and slide lubrication as directed for the installed mill. Report low levels, damage or a maintenance need rather than adjusting the machine's sliding fits yourself.
  • Complete the installed mill's guard and emergency-stop checks from [[equipment-operating-procedure]] and [[machine-hazards]].

A rotating cutter and a moving table create different hazards

The cutter can cut or catch what touches it; the table can close a gap against the column, vise or clamps. Keep clear of both the cutting area and the table's travel. CNC motion follows the program, so a pause in motion is not permission to reach in.

Use [[machine-hazards]] for stopping before measurements and for isolation before adjustments or maintenance. Use [[metal-chips]] for chip removal, including the required controls if compressed air is used.

If a tool breaks or the work comes loose, step away

In our labs, step away if a tool breaks or the workpiece becomes loose. Stop the mill if it is safe to do so; do not endanger yourself to reach a control. Notify the instructor in every case.

If injured, ask for help and follow [[conduct-and-emergency]]. For normal shutdown use the installed mill's procedure and [[equipment-operating-procedure]]; the Tormach sequence is in [[mill-setup-checklist]].
